Court Rules & Amenities

1 Booking & Payment

  • Full payment is required to secure your booking.
  • No cancellation, no refund, and no rebooking once confirmed.
  • Please arrive on time and respect your reserved schedule.

2 Court Usage & Safety

  • Bring your own pickleball paddle and balls.
  • Wear proper sportswear and non-marking court shoes.
  • Children must be supervised by an adult. CCTV is active 24/7.

3 Street Parking Rules

  • Car parking: Up to 2 cars only (subject to availability).
  • Motorcycle parking: Up to 5 motorcycles only.
  • Please inform us in advance if bringing a car so our helper can assist you. Do not block driveways.

4 Amenities & Food Policy

  • Comfort room, shower area, and free drinking water available.
  • Waiting & resting area (up to 15 seats) + 1 court helper on duty.
  • All food & drinks allowed (dispose of trash properly). Strictly no smoking/vaping.
